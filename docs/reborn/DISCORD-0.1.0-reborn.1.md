# 📊 Azeroth Stats Reborn — Your Runs, Your Progress

**Azeroth Stats is now available for Azeroth Survivors Reborn!** Keep your run history, understand your builds and track your progress in one place.

⚔️ **Analyse your combat** — spell breakdowns, recent damage/healing windows and custom combat segments.
📖 **Explore your runs** — search, filters, favorites and side-by-side comparisons.
🧩 **Review your builds** — spells, ranks, equipment bonuses, encounters and build changes.
🏆 **Track your progress** — records, trends and Nemesis statistics.
🎨 **Make it yours** — five visual themes, scaling, opacity, language settings and an optional compact HUD.

**Smarter local backups**
Shared storage is enabled for new players. Existing profiles can migrate directly in the addon, with a verified local safety copy, recovery and a return to classic storage — no external tools needed.

**Works alongside Azeroth FieldKit Reborn.** Your data stays local, and your original Azeroth Stats history remains separate.

📥 **Download:** https://github.com/Maidenquinn/azeroth-stats-reborn/releases/latest
Extract `AzerothStatsReborn` into `Interface/AddOns`, then type **`/azstatsr`**.
For existing profiles: **Settings → Manage backups → Backup storage**, or **`/azstatsr storage`**.
